Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about InterviewAI platform capabilities.

How does the AI evaluate my interview responses?

Gemini AI analyzes your response across 5 core competencies: Technical Accuracy, Communication Clarity, Confidence Level, Grammar, and Relevance to Role. You receive instant numerical scores and actionable bullet points for improvement.

Is my webcam video stored on any external server?

No. Your webcam video feed is processed entirely in your browser using standard HTML5 MediaStream API. No raw video files are uploaded or saved to external servers.

Can I generate questions tailored to my uploaded resume?

Yes! In the Interview Setup form, enable "Resume-Tailored Questions". The AI will parse your uploaded resume and formulate custom questions targeting your actual projects, tech stack, and achievements.

What interview modes are available?

InterviewAI offers three distinct modes: Text Interview (typed responses), Voice Interview (speech-to-text dictation & voice question reader), and Video Interview (live webcam stream with real-time speech transcription).
